ABOUT

Practical buying guides for college life.

CampusKit is a student-focused guide for comparing useful products and planning a simple college setup.

Our approach

We aim to explain what matters before a student buys something: practical features, portability, compatibility, price considerations and trade-offs.

What we don't do

We don't claim to have personally tested a product unless we actually have. Product prices and availability can change, so readers should check the retailer before purchasing.

Why the site exists

College shopping can be confusing when every product page makes the same promises. CampusKit is designed to make the decision easier to understand.
