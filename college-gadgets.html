10 Useful Gadgets for College Students

A starting list of practical gadgets. You don't need to buy everything on it.

Quick note: This guide is designed to help you compare options. Product prices, specifications and availability can change.

What to look for

  • Laptop stand — useful for a more adjustable desk setup.
  • Wireless mouse — useful if you prefer a mouse to a trackpad.
  • USB hub — useful when your laptop has limited ports; check exact port compatibility.
  • Laptop sleeve — useful for carrying a laptop separately from a backpack.
  • Desk lamp — useful for hostel or late-evening study.
  • Cable organizer — helps keep chargers and cables together.
  • Power bank — useful for compatible devices when away from a socket; check capacity and airline/travel rules if relevant.
  • Keyboard — useful for students who prefer a separate typing setup.
  • Headphones/earphones — useful for lectures or focused work, depending on campus rules.
  • Small desk organizer — useful when desk space is limited.

How to choose

Start with the problem you are trying to solve, then compare the features that directly affect it. Avoid paying for features you will not use. Always verify the latest specifications and price on the retailer's page before purchasing.

Before you buy

  • Check compatibility with your existing devices.
  • Read the current product specifications and warranty information.
  • Compare the total price, including any delivery charges shown by the retailer.
  • Check recent customer feedback for recurring practical issues.
